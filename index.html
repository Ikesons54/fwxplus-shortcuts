How to install

  1. Tap an Add button above on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. In the Shortcuts app, tap Add Shortcut.
  3. Open it from the Shortcuts app. Allow access to Photos, Files or Notes when asked.

Questions

Do I need to pay or sign up?

No. These three shortcuts are free, and you don’t need an account.

Why does it ask for permissions?

Each shortcut only asks for what it needs: Notes for the expense log, Photos for the QR code and PDF, and Files to save the PDF.

It says I can’t open a shortcut from an untrusted source.

Open Settings, go to Shortcuts, and turn on Allow Sharing Large Amounts of Data or Private Sharing if your iPhone asks. Then tap the Add button again.

Where do I report a problem?

Message us on WhatsApp and tell us your iOS version and which shortcut failed.
