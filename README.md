# FWXplus Shortcuts

**Free iPhone and iPad shortcuts that save you time, built by Frameworks x+.**

FWXplus Shortcuts is a growing collection of simple, useful automations for Apple's Shortcuts app. Each one does a single job well, runs in a few taps, and needs no extra app and no account. Everything runs on your own device.

**Landing page:** https://YOUR-ikesons54.github.io/fwxplus-shortcuts/

## Why this project exists

Most people never use the Shortcuts app, even though it is already on their phone. The shortcuts people search for are often buried in long lists or tied to apps they don't want. This project collects practical shortcuts in one place, explains what each one does in plain language, and keeps them free and easy to install.

Frameworks x+ builds digital products and tools, and this collection is one of them. New shortcuts will be added over time.

## The shortcuts

### Quick Expense Logger
Record what you spend in about five seconds, without opening a spreadsheet or a budgeting app.

- Enter the amount
- Pick a category (Food, Transport, Airtime/Data, Bills, or Other)
- Add an optional note
- The entry is saved with the date in your Notes app

It is a good fit for anyone who wants to track daily spending but finds full budgeting apps too slow.

### QR Generator
Type any text or paste a link, and get a QR code saved straight to your Photos. Useful for sharing a website, a contact detail, a Wi-Fi note, a payment link, or an event page, whether you share it on screen or print it.

### Photos to PDF
Select as many photos as you need, give the file a name, and get a single PDF saved to the Files app. It works well for receipts, forms, school documents, and copies of paperwork that you need to send as one file.

## How to install

1. Open the landing page on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Tap the **Add** button for the shortcut you want.
3. In the Shortcuts app, tap **Add Shortcut**.
4. Open the shortcut from the Shortcuts app and allow access to Photos, Files, or Notes when asked.

Each shortcut asks only for the permissions it needs: Notes for the expense log, Photos for QR codes and PDFs, and Files for saving the PDF.

## Troubleshooting

**Your iPhone says it can't open a shortcut from an untrusted source.**
Open Settings, go to Shortcuts, and turn on the sharing option your device offers. Then tap the Add button again.

**A shortcut doesn't save where you expected.**
Run it once and check the Notes, Photos, or Files app, depending on the shortcut. If it still fails, open the shortcut in the Shortcuts app and make sure the save location is still available on your device.

**Something else isn't working.**
Open an issue in this repository and include your iOS version and the name of the shortcut. That makes it much faster to find the cause.
